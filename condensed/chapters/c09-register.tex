\chapter{Forecasts and Dashboard}
\label{c:register}

The full edition carries a graded evidence ledger, a machine-readable claim register, and the two tables reproduced here \fullapp{C, I}. The register states grades; these tables state bets. Every forecast is a scenario judgment [S] with a probability, a horizon, and a falsifier, so that a reader in 2029 can mechanically score the book; where the full edition prints a skeptic's figure alongside the author's, it appears in brackets. A reader who checks the dashboard quarterly does not need to re-read the book to know whether it is aging well. Four terms in the tables are the full edition's: ``stage two'' is the second rung of its proof ladder for the integrated-CPU claim (parity on AI serving benchmarks after the settled HPL/HPCG rung); the ``bandwidth knee'' is the socket memory bandwidth below which the capacity-first architecture's efficiency falls off (modeled near 5\,TB/s); ``VLA'' is a vision-language-action model, the policy layer of an embodied system; and the ``Max-tier template'' is the pattern of opening a flagship's weights while gating commercial use above a revenue threshold.
