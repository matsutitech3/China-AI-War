\chapter{Forecasts and Dashboard}
\label{c:register}

The full edition carries a graded evidence ledger, a machine-readable claim register, and the two tables reproduced here \fullapp{D, J}. Every forecast is a scenario judgment [S] with a probability, a horizon and a falsifier, so that a reader in 2029 can mechanically score the book; a skeptic's figure, where the full edition prints one, appears in brackets. The cycle of 24~September--6~November is collected in \fullapp{H}. Two terms are the full edition's: ``stage two'' is the second rung of its proof ladder for the integrated-CPU claim (parity on AI serving benchmarks after the settled HPL/HPCG rung), and the ``Max-tier template'' is the pattern of opening a flagship's weights while gating commercial use above a revenue threshold.
