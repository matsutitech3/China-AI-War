\chapter{The Strategy in Whole}
\label{c:assembled}

\begin{glance}
The Chinese AI strategy is the playbook of Chapter~\ref{c:precedents} run on a fourth industry, with one adaptation that makes it faster: the rent layer under attack is digital, so the commoditizing move---openness---costs nothing to reproduce and propagates at the speed of a download. Placed on the playbook---a map drawn before the territory---every one of the nine moves is occupied at a recognizable stage: one of the six steps is complete, one complete at home and contested abroad, three trending, and the endgame barely begun. The three properties on which the playbook has failed before---the moving frontier, ecosystem lock, trust---are exactly the ones the elements cannot settle from inside; and Chapter~\ref{c:mirror} adds that the moves catalogued here are not, in 2026, uniquely Chinese. \full{9}
\end{glance}

\section{Where each element sits}

\emph{Target selection} chose the discontinuity where the incumbent's advantage is about to be stranded: HBM-centric superclusters, closed frontier weights, a licensed instruction set. \emph{Demand before supply} came by mandate rather than feed-in tariff---state-funded data centers restricted to domestic silicon, western hub electricity discounted only for approved domestic chips~\cite{cac-ban,datacenter-mandate}. \emph{Overcapacity as strategy} is under way in DRAM and compute halls, with state-subsidized compute at 20--30\% utilization against a 60\% target and the losses landing where they landed in solar; but the accelerator layer is still supply-bound by HBM, and the model-layer price war runs without a visible rent-recapture plan, which Chapter~\ref{c:strategies} calls the strategy's soft underbelly. \emph{Selection, not planning} is visible in a model layer where ten laboratories release at frontier scale. \emph{Commoditizing the adversary's rent layer} is what Chapter~\ref{c:elements} documents: open weights, harness, ISA, interconnect and framework specifications at marginal or zero price, aimed at CUDA, NVLink, x86/Arm licensing and the metered API~\cite{action-plan,riscv-policy}. And the \emph{endgame} has begun on consolidation---the anti-involution campaign---not yet on gatekeeping, where batteries and rare earths supply the template~\cite{mofcom-battery-controls}. Three interlocks are the strategy. The model and the compute are the coupling P1$\times$P2, which turns a download into a deployment. The watt is the weld: a tariff instrument paying only for domestic silicon converts an energy surplus into hardware volume and hardware volume into tokens priced below the Western marginal cost. And the semiconductor is the floor, incomplete at exactly one layer---HBM---which is why the memory arbitrage exists at all: the architecture was chosen so that the one input the chokepoint strategy still holds is the one it needs least.


\section{What the assembly licenses}

It licenses the claim that the AI campaign is a coherent execution of the strategy that won three technology wars, further along than a layer-by-layer reading suggests, its remaining gaps engineered around rather than closed on the West's terms. It forbids the claim that the outcome is settled: rent destruction has run furthest and its completion is contested, the integration test that would move sovereign compute from trending to done has not been passed, and Chapter~\ref{c:precedents}'s three resisting properties are the three the deployment layer is made of.
